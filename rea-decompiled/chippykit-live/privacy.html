Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 May 2026

ChippyKit ("we", "us", "our") is operated by MB3 Carpentry & Maintenance, an Australian sole trader business. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the ChippyKit app or website, how we use it, and how we protect it.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), even though as a small business we're not strictly required to. We think it's the right thing to do.

What we collect

When you create a ChippyKit account, we collect:

  • Your email address — used for account login and to send you important updates about the app
  • Your password — stored securely (encrypted, never in plain text)

We also store, against your account:

  • Your subscription status — whether you're on the free tier, in a trial, or subscribed to Pro

Your work stays on your phone. The jobs, quotes, invoices and templates you create — including client names, site addresses and amounts — are stored on your own device, not on our servers. We never receive them and cannot see them. The same goes for your saved calculator inputs, your business profile and logo, and your in-app preferences.

That means two things worth knowing. Your client details are private to your device, which is how we think it should be. But it also means they aren't backed up by us: if you lose the phone or delete the app, that work is gone, so keep your own copies of anything that matters.

If you purchase ChippyKit Pro or the founders deal, the payment is processed by Stripe, a global payment provider. Stripe collects your name, email, and payment details directly — we never see or store your card numbers. You can read Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/au/privacy.

How we use your information

We only use your information to:

  • Provide the ChippyKit app and its features to you
  • Process payments and provide receipts
  • Send you essential account and product updates (e.g. when the app launches, or when there are important changes)
  • Respond to support questions you send us
  • Improve the app based on how it's actually used

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not send marketing emails unless you've explicitly asked us to.

Where your information is stored

Your account information — your email address, your password and your subscription status — is stored on cloud infrastructure managed by reputable third-party providers. We use industry-standard encryption to protect it both in transit (when it travels between your device and our servers) and at rest. The jobs, quotes and invoices you create are not sent to us at all; they stay on your device.

Some of our service providers may store data outside Australia. By using ChippyKit, you consent to your information being transferred and stored in countries other than Australia, where data protection laws may differ. We only use providers who maintain strong data protection standards.

Who we share information with

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run the app:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Cloud hosting providers — for storing and serving the app
  • Email delivery providers — for sending essential emails to you

We do not share your information with any other parties unless required by law (e.g. a valid legal request from Australian authorities).

Your data, your choice

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct any information that's wrong or out of date
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mb3constructions@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe (usually within 7 days).

How long we keep your data

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can delete it yourself at any time from the Profile tab in the app — that happens immediately, not after a waiting period, and it also erases the jobs, quotes and invoices held on your device. The one exception is records we're legally required to keep: payment and transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by Australian tax law, with your email address stripped from them.

Cookies and tracking

The ChippyKit website uses minimal tracking. We don't use third-party advertising cookies. We may use basic analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site (e.g. which pages get viewed), but this data is aggregated and not used to identify individual people.

Children's privacy

ChippyKit is designed for working tradespeople and is not directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Data breaches

If a data breach occurs that's likely to result in serious harm to you, we'll notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as soon as practicable, in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll let you know by email or by a notice in the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page tells you when it was last changed.
