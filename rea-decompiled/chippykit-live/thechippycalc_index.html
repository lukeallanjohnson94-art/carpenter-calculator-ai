About The Chippy Calc

The Chippy Calc was born from a simple idea: to create a construction calculator that not only provides accurate measurements but also visualizes them. As a qualified carpenter, I understand the challenges of translating numbers into real-world applications.

This app bridges this gap by offering precise calculations alongside scaled diagrams, making it easier to understand and implement measurements on-site. Whether you're a professional contractor or a DIY enthusiast, The Chippy Calc simplifies complex construction calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about The Chippy Calc app and its features.

What is The Chippy Calc?

The Chippy Calc is a specialized construction calculator app designed for builders, carpenters, and DIY enthusiasts. It offers precise calculations and visual diagrams for various construction tasks like balustrade spacing, stair calculations, rafter measurements, and more.

Is The Chippy Calc available for both iOS and Android?

Yes, The Chippy Calc is available for download on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play Store) devices.

Are there free calculators available in the app?

Yes, The Chippy Calc offers several free calculators including the Right Angle Triangle, Hip/Valley Pitch, and Check Square features. Premium calculators are available with a one-time purchase.

How accurate are the calculations?

The Chippy Calc provides highly accurate calculations designed to meet industry standards. All formulas have been thoroughly tested and verified by construction professionals.

Can I save my calculations for future reference?

Yes, The Chippy Calc allows you to save your calculations for easy reference later. This is particularly useful for ongoing projects or when you need to revisit measurements.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, once downloaded, The Chippy Calc works completely offline. No internet connection is required to use any of the calculators or features.

Is there a desktop version available?

Currently, The Chippy Calc is only available as a mobile app for iOS and Android devices. We're considering desktop options for future releases based on user demand.

How do I get help if I'm having trouble with a calculation?

The app includes detailed help sections for each calculator. If you need additional assistance, you can contact our support team at support@thechippycalc.com.
