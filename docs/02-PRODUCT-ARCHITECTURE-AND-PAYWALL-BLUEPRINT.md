## 1. The "Blue-Balls" High-Conversion Freemium Paywall Architecture

### Why Standard Calculator Paywalls Fail vs Why Ours Converts on Site
Most apps either lock the whole calculator behind a dead padlock icon (so the user uninstalls immediately without seeing the value) OR give away all the numbers for free (so nobody ever upgrades).

Our **"Blue-Balls" Jobsite Conversion Engine** gives every apprentice and carpenter the **100% Free Hook** while gating the exact **Saw-Blade & Marking-Out Execution Callouts** right when the timber is on the horses and the drop-saw is in their hand:

| **1.
